EPA Toxics Release Inventory — what industrial facilities report releasing near your zip code

National Overview

Under the Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act, industrial facilities must report toxic chemical releases to the EPA annually. This data covers 2023 reports.

Source: U.S. EPA Toxics Release Inventory (TRI), 2023 reporting year. ZIP code centroids from U.S. Census Bureau.

What TRI covers: Facilities with 10+ employees that manufacture, process, or use listed toxic chemicals above threshold quantities. Smaller facilities and non-listed chemicals are not included. Absence of a facility near you does not mean no toxic releases occur.

What this is: An informational tool. Release quantities are self-reported estimates, not measurements. This is not a health risk assessment and should not be treated as one. Consult your local health department or EPA regional office for health guidance.

Data from EPA TRI Program. Pipeline and source on GitHub.
